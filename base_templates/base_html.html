1. Overview

Arens de Lledó is situated on the border of Aragon and Catalonia along the Algars River. The village preserves significant medieval urban topology, characterized by narrow roadways and stone masonry structures dating back to the 14th century.

2. Archival Discoveries

Recent field surveys recovered administrative logs detailing regional trade routes and water management agreements. Key findings include:

  • Municipal registry logs from 1892 to 1910.
  • Irrigation rights document for the Algars basin.
  • Photographic plates capturing the primary plaza restoration.
"The bridge connection remains the vital artery between agricultural plots and market access during high-water seasons."

3. Current Preservation Objectives

Efforts focus on digitizing physical registers into structured database formats, ensuring long-term resilience against degradation and physical loss.
